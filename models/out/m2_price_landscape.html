Why the textbook Tobit fails here (specification test)

A Type-I Tobit stretches to sigma = 1.43 in logs: censoring in this marketplace is participation-driven (C19 recency suppression), not low-valuation-driven, so the single-sigma latent collapses two orders of magnitude below the sub-reserve demand the lake actually logged (median latent $0.18 vs $32.25 observed). The bids shown were never used in training either model; against them the survival landscape's expected value ranks unsold leads as well as the Tobit does (Spearman 0.38 vs 0.38) while staying calibrated in dollars.
