Recommended schedule

Coordinate descent over per-tier multipliers on the replayed waterfall, bounded below at 20% of the deployed reserve (near-zero reserves make single-bidder sales clear near zero under second-price rules); raised-floor demand at unobserved deeper tiers is hot-deck imputed (conservative -- see the model card). Deep-tier multipliers sit at the bound: below it the revenue curve is flat.
